# Global Funding Application Charter

## Non-negotiable applicant principles

- The encyclopedia remains available to the public at no charge.
- No grant, sponsorship, award, or research funding may transfer ownership of the encyclopedia, application, repository, domain, corpus, or core project assets without the owner's explicit written consent after independent review.
- No funder may receive operational control, merge control, administrative credentials, or unilateral authority over scientific/content decisions unless explicitly and separately accepted in writing.
- No funding agreement may require a paid API, hosted SaaS, subscription, or vendor lock-in as a core runtime dependency.
- Funding does not override software, model, weights, voice, dataset, content, or provenance licenses.
- Funded technology remains a candidate until it passes the project's security, rights, Arabic-quality, resource, provenance, and human-review gates.
- Personal financial contribution is not assumed. Any cost-sharing or matching requirement must be disclosed before submission and rejected when it requires unaffordable personal contribution.
- Optional donations may exist as secondary support, but public access to the encyclopedia is never conditional on payment.
- Any reuse of grant funds for other public-benefit work must be allowed by the funding agreement and applicable law; otherwise funds remain restricted to the approved budget.

## What a funder may receive

Subject to the specific agreement, the funder may receive progress reports, measurable outcomes, research publications, open-source/open-access deliverables, public attribution, and defined rights to use agreed outputs. These rights must be explicit and must not be treated as ownership or project control unless separately agreed.

## Application review gate

Before accepting any award, review:

1. Applicant eligibility and whether an individual, nonprofit, university, fiscal sponsor, or other legal entity must receive the funds.
2. Total award, payment schedule, eligible costs, indirect costs, taxes, reporting, audit, and any matching/cost-share requirements.
3. Intellectual-property ownership, background IP, foreground/results IP, licensing, publication, access, and data rights.
4. Any governance, steering, approval, exclusivity, or control rights granted to the funder.
5. Any requirement to use a particular vendor, hosted API, cloud, model, dataset, or platform.
6. Double-funding and conflict restrictions.
7. Termination, clawback, repayment, or change-of-control clauses.
8. Cross-border payment, banking, tax, invoicing, and legal-compliance requirements.

## External communication position

The project is open to compatible funding because funding can accelerate public-benefit work such as Arabic OCR, ASR, search, semantic knowledge, document processing, security, provenance, benchmarks, accessibility, and infrastructure. The project is not seeking to sell ownership or surrender independence in exchange for funding.
