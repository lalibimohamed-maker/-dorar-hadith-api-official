# موسوعة دينُ اللّه — Global Deep Learning Intelligence Strategy 2026

## Mission
Build a continuously improving, worldwide, multilingual knowledge-and-learning system in which the corpus is the durable foundation and learning intelligence helps people understand, compare, remember, research, and transfer knowledge without compromising source integrity, rights, provenance, or the continuity of PDF acquisition.

## Non-negotiable architecture rule
**Learning enrichment must never become a gate on corpus acquisition.** A book can be acquired, preserved, validated, and recorded even when OCR, semantic enrichment, a knowledge graph, a visualization, or a learning module is unavailable. Enrichment is asynchronous and additive.

## 1. Global research intelligence
Continuously discover methods and evidence from learning science, cognitive psychology, educational technology, information retrieval, semantic search, knowledge graphs, intelligent tutoring systems, adaptive learning, educational visualization, multilingual NLP, and human-centred AI.

Research loop: discover -> screen -> evaluate evidence -> extract method -> prototype -> measure -> integrate -> monitor -> revise.

## 2. Mass practice and learning processes
1. Diagnose prior knowledge.
2. Identify prerequisites and misconceptions.
3. Present a concise source-grounded explanation.
4. Show a worked example where appropriate.
5. Ask for retrieval rather than immediate rereading.
6. Give corrective feedback and explain why.
7. Revisit knowledge with spacing.
8. Interleave related but discriminable concepts.
9. Require self-explanation and teach-back.
10. Test transfer to a new context.
11. Record confidence separately from correctness.
12. Adapt the next activity to demonstrated need.

Retrieval is not merely a quiz feature; feedback, task design, retention interval, complexity, and transfer conditions must be considered.

## 3. Knowledge architecture
Create a source-grounded knowledge layer connecting:
source -> passage/page -> claim -> concept -> relation -> scholarly position -> uncertainty -> learner activity.

Support concept graphs, prerequisite graphs, timelines, comparison matrices, evidence maps, and visual learning paths.

## 4. Global multilingual intelligence
Search and discovery operate across Arabic and world languages while preserving original language and provenance. Translation is an access layer, not a replacement for canonical source text. Quran canonical Arabic remains unchanged.

Global source intelligence should learn reusable source families rather than produce one-time search results: national and university libraries, manuscript repositories, official Islamic institutions, open digital libraries, scholarly indexes, regional-language repositories, audio archives, video archives, and educational repositories.

## 5. Adaptive learner model
Track only what is needed for useful adaptation: demonstrated mastery, prerequisite gaps, retrieval strength, recent errors, confidence calibration, preferred language/interface, learning history, and source-reading progress. Avoid unnecessary sensitive profiling and remain explainable.

## 6. Scholar and evidence safeguards
For disputed or interpretive subjects distinguish primary evidence, transmitted report, scholarly interpretation, later synthesis, system inference, and unresolved uncertainty. Never manufacture consensus; expose relevant positions, evidence, provenance, confidence, and disagreement.

## 7. Worship learning and Quran recitation
Learning features may guide practice in worship and Quran studies, but authoritative correction claims require validated technical evidence. Recitation remains Arabic even when the interface language changes. No fabricated Tajweed or phoneme-level correction is permitted.

## 8. Visual and interactive learning models
Future educational components may include interactive concept maps, knowledge graphs, prerequisite ladders, chronological timelines, madhhab comparison matrices, narrator/source relationship graphs, tafsir comparison views, evidence chains, book-to-concept maps, interactive worked examples, and cumulative review dashboards.

These are asynchronous enrichment layers and never acquisition blockers.

## 9. Free-first engineering
The core corpus and learning architecture should remain usable with free/open tooling whenever practical. Paid AI inference is not a prerequisite for core acquisition. Deterministic local tooling, cached source metadata, reusable indexes, and open standards are preferred.

## 10. Continuous evaluation
Evaluate major learning methods against measurable outcomes: retention, delayed retrieval, transfer, error correction, confidence calibration, source comprehension, research quality, and ability to explain or teach back. A feature is not successful merely because it looks educational.

## 11. Human-centred AI
AI should expand access and capability without replacing human judgment, scholarship, or accountability. Inclusion, equity, transparency, privacy, and human review remain design principles.

## 12. Long-term operating model
Corpus acquisition -> rights/provenance -> preservation -> text/OCR -> semantic indexing -> knowledge graph -> learning objects -> practice -> assessment -> learner adaptation -> research feedback -> method improvement -> back into the learning engine.

The corpus remains authoritative infrastructure; learning intelligence is a continuously improving layer above it.

## Strategic outcome
The target is not merely a larger Islamic website. The target is a durable, worldwide, source-grounded Islamic knowledge corpus with an adaptive learning environment that improves understanding through evidence-informed practice while preserving scholarly distinctions, provenance, rights, and the ability of Rechercher to keep acquiring books continuously.
