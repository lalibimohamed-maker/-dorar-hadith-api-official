# First Evidence-Driven Scientific-Religious Video Prototype

## Subject
Ant communication and the Quranic witness in Surah An-Naml 27:18.

## Scientific framing
The prototype does **not** claim that ants speak like humans. It presents the narrower, sourced claim that ants use multiple communication channels, including chemical, tactile and vibrational signalling. A 2021 Scientific Reports study found that stridulatory signals in *Crematogaster scutellaris* varied with behavioural context; a 2017 review surveys acoustic signalling in ant societies.

## Quranic layer
The prototype includes Surah An-Naml 27:18 as the Quranic witness. The Arabic source layer is kept verbatim. Translation is treated as a separate asset. Tanzil's text license allows verbatim copying with attribution and prohibits changing the text.

## Why this first prototype is an animatic
The current repository can deterministically prove the evidence graph, timing, composition, Arabic text layer, rights boundary and rendering path without downloading or embedding third-party footage. The workflow therefore renders an original vector animatic as a real MP4 artifact.

This is intentionally **not yet** the final photorealistic documentary renderer. The production pipeline will replace selected vector scenes with rights-cleared scientific footage or original generated scenes after rights and provenance checks pass, then add a rights-cleared recitation, word alignment, multilingual meanings, color-fidelity processing and the 48K-class mastering path.

## Rights and provenance rules
- No third-party video is embedded in the prototype.
- No synthetic imitation of a named Quran reciter is used.
- The prototype stays silent until a rights-cleared recitation is selected.
- Every future media asset must carry source, license evidence, usage scope and checksum before it can enter a production timeline.
- The scientific-religious relationship remains an evidence/interpretive layer; the system must not promote an interpretive link into a scientific fact automatically.
