# Free-first Engine Control Plane — 2026

This registry is the control plane for selecting open/self-hosted engines without making any paid API or subscription a core dependency.

An engine moves through `DISCOVERED → LICENSE_REVIEW → SECURITY_REVIEW → BENCHMARKING → RUNTIME_VERIFIED → APPROVED → ENABLED`. Unknown or insufficiently evidenced capabilities remain blocked or quarantined.

The registry covers OCR, speech/TTS, search, security, media, and cross-device display. Engine code, pretrained models/weights, dependencies, and platform adapters are reviewed separately because they can carry different licensing and operational conditions.

The resolution policy is open-ended: native, 4K, 8K, 10K, 12K, 16K, 24K, and future targets may be represented. A derived/AI-reconstructed output must never be presented as native merely because its dimensions are large.

The registry does not silently install privileged software, expose screens to the internet, replace authoritative Corpus content, or promote external discovery results to scholarly authority.
