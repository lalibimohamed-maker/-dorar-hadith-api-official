# Rechercher — 2029–2030+

Beyond V12, the proposed V13–V20 program focuses on knowledge continuity, living scholarly graphs, reproducible research, human-AI scholar workspaces, federated libraries, personal lifelong mastery memory, collective review, and a global knowledge observatory.

Beyond V20, V21+ remains exploratory: decentralized preservation, multimodal archival reconstruction, machine-assisted manuscript collation, cross-corpus ontology alignment, simulation-based research training, and human-approved autonomous research agents.

These are strategic horizons, not implemented features. Each must pass source, rights, provenance, security, evaluation, human-review, failure-isolation, and rollback gates before implementation.

The immutable boundaries remain: acquisition is independent; original PDFs and canonical Quran Arabic are not modified; AI is not a mufti; and rights/provenance remain authoritative.
