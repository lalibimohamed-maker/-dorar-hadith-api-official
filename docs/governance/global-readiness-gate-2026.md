# Global Readiness Gate — Deen Allah Encyclopedia 2026

This gate applies to new technologies, engines, models, datasets, media assets, and integrations.

## Mandatory evidence

Every proposed capability must document:

- purpose and owner;
- license and usage terms;
- provenance/source;
- security review;
- privacy/data-flow review;
- benchmark and language coverage;
- primary engine and fallback where practical;
- health check/observability;
- rollback or kill switch;
- backup/recovery impact;
- accessibility impact.

## Corpus isolation

- No technology integration may silently modify authoritative Corpus records.
- Scientific, religious, or historical claims generated by AI remain non-authoritative until independently reviewed.
- Search normalization, translation, OCR, transcription, and summarization are presentation/indexing layers unless separately verified.

## Resilience

A capability must degrade safely when its primary engine fails. Text remains available when audio fails; keyword search remains available when semantic search fails; cached/offline functionality remains usable within its declared scope.

## Rights gate

Unknown, conflicting, or non-production-compatible rights status blocks production use. Public metadata does not imply permission to mirror protected full text, recordings, images, models, or datasets.

## Privacy gate

Microphone, camera, location, analytics, and uploaded media require explicit permissions where applicable. Local processing is preferred. Raw audio/video retention is off by default unless a documented feature requires it.

## Internationalization and accessibility

Support RTL/LTR, language identification, mixed-language content, WCAG 2.2 targets, captions/transcripts, keyboard navigation, screen readers, reduced motion, and language-specific sign-language resources.

## Supply chain

Dependencies and artifacts should be traceable through SBOM/provenance and verified signatures where supported. CI must reject known policy violations rather than silently accepting them.

## Lifecycle

Every adopted component is classified as `ADOPT`, `TRIAL`, `ASSESS`, `HOLD`, or `DEPRECATED` and periodically re-evaluated for security, maintenance, performance, licensing, and language coverage.

## Merge rule

A PR introducing a new production capability must provide the evidence above and pass repository-required checks. No direct writes to protected branches are permitted.
