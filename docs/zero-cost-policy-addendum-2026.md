# Zero-Cost-First Addendum

The project is designed so that poverty or lack of a payment card is never a prerequisite for accessing the core encyclopedia.

### User-facing commitment

- Core reading is free.
- Core search is free.
- Approved downloads are free when rights permit redistribution.
- The web/PWA path does not require an app-store developer membership.
- Supported localized presentations are free to access.

### Engineering commitment

- No single paid provider is required for core operation.
- No paid API key is required for core reading/search.
- Free/open-source/self-hostable alternatives are preferred.
- Provider quotas and terms are monitored.
- When a free quota is exhausted, the system degrades to cached/local/offline/queued paths instead of silently generating a bill.
- Paid infrastructure may exist only as an optional accelerator, mirror, or volunteer/community-provided distribution channel and may not become the sole path for core access without an explicit governance change.

### Accuracy of the promise

The encyclopedia does not promise that every third-party infrastructure service will remain free forever or unlimited. It promises an architecture that does not make such a service a mandatory financial dependency.

### App-store reality

Official stores can impose developer-account fees. Therefore store publication is a distribution option, not the definition of the application or the prerequisite for web/PWA access. The project will use free channels where eligible and may accept volunteer/community sponsorship of store memberships.

### Rights reality

"Free to access" and "free to redistribute" are not synonyms. Every book, image, audio recording, video, translation, font, dataset and other third-party asset passes a rights gate before re-hosting or bundling.
