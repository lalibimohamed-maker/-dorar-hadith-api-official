# Rechercher — Long-Horizon Intelligence Roadmap V5–V20+

Strategic roadmap only. V4 is the current implementation increment. V5+ are not implemented until code, tests, security review, and merge are complete.

## 2027–2028
- V5 Cognitive Learning Intelligence: knowledge-gap diagnosis, misconception recovery, confidence calibration, cognitive-load adaptation, transfer and mastery forecasting.
- V6 Global Multilingual Intelligence: language-neutral concepts, terminology alignment, cross-language evidence linking, multilingual search and learning.
- V7 Research & Evidence Graph: claim/evidence/source/attribution/chain/scholar/edition/manuscript graphs with contradiction-aware navigation.
- V8 Adaptive Learning: explainable individualized paths, diagnostic-to-mastery loops, teach-back, Socratic guidance, research apprenticeship.
- V9 Multimodal & Recitation Intelligence: PDF/image/audio/video alignment, Quran memorization and tajweed candidate feedback with validated-evaluator boundaries.
- V10 Global Islamic Research Network: integrated research, learning, source navigation, and reproducible research dossiers.
- V11 Open Islamic Learning Infrastructure: open schemas, export/import, interoperable learning records, reproducible datasets where rights permit, and community tooling.
- V12 Global Islamic Knowledge Commons: distributed, provenance-aware, rights-aware collaboration across libraries, researchers, teachers, and learners.

## 2029–2030+
- V13 Knowledge Continuity Engine: long-term preservation, version lineage, citation permanence, source replacement detection, institutional memory.
- V14 Living Scholarly Graph: review-gated temporal knowledge graph with explicit uncertainty.
- V15 Research Reproducibility Lab: reproducible search trails, evidence bundles, notebooks, manifests, claim-to-source audit.
- V16 Human–AI Scholar Workspace: source reading, argument mapping, takhrij workspace, comparison matrices, review queues, collaborative annotation.
- V17 Federated Islamic Libraries Layer: interoperable discovery across independent collections while local ownership and rights remain authoritative.
- V18 Personal Knowledge & Mastery Memory: user-controlled lifelong learning memory, portable learner profile, offline operation, deletion/export controls, privacy-preserving synchronization.
- V19 Collective Intelligence & Peer Review: disagreement maps, contested-state tracking, evidence-weighted collaboration without turning consensus into truth.
- V20 Global Islamic Knowledge Observatory: longitudinal mapping of editions, manuscripts, translations, scholarly claims, curricula, learning outcomes, source gaps, preservation risks.

## V21+
Exploratory areas: decentralized preservation, multimodal archival reconstruction, machine-assisted manuscript collation, cross-corpus ontology alignment, simulation-based research training, and human-approved autonomous research agents.

## Permanent boundaries
Acquisition remains independent and cannot be blocked by learning intelligence. Original PDFs and canonical Quran Arabic remain immutable. Rights, provenance, source identity, and scholarly disagreement remain first-class. AI is an assistant, not a mufti. AI-generated educational artifacts remain explicitly labeled until reviewed. Free/open-source-first remains the default.

Every future feature requires source/provenance tests, rights tests, security tests, deterministic schema tests, effectiveness evaluation, human review where authoritative interpretation is involved, failure isolation, rollback/fallback, and documented limitations.
