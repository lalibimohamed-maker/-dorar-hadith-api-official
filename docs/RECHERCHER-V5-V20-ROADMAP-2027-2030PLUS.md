# Rechercher — Long-Horizon Intelligence Roadmap V5–V20+

## Status
Strategic roadmap. V4 remains the current implementation increment; V5+ are planned capability horizons and must not be described as implemented until their code, tests, security review, and merge are complete.

## Non-negotiable invariants
- PDF acquisition remains independent and must never be blocked by learning intelligence.
- Original PDFs remain immutable.
- Canonical Quran Arabic remains immutable; translations are separate layers.
- Rights, provenance, source identity, and scholarly disagreement are first-class data.
- AI is an assistant, not a mufti or an authority.
- AI-generated educational artifacts remain explicitly labeled until reviewed.
- Free/open-source-first remains the default.
- Every future capability requires measurable evaluation and rollback/fallback behavior.

## V5–V12: 2027–2028
- V5 Cognitive Learning Intelligence: knowledge-gap diagnosis, misconception recovery, calibration, cognitive-load adaptation, transfer and mastery forecasting.
- V6 Global Multilingual Intelligence: language-neutral concepts, terminology alignment, cross-language evidence linking, multilingual search and learning.
- V7 Research & Evidence Graph: claim/evidence/source/attribution/chain/scholar/edition/manuscript graphs with contradiction-aware navigation.
- V8 Adaptive Learning: explainable individualized paths, diagnostic-to-mastery loops, teach-back, Socratic guidance, research apprenticeship.
- V9 Multimodal & Recitation Intelligence: text/PDF/image/audio/video alignment, Quran memorization and tajweed candidate feedback with validated-evaluator boundaries.
- V10 Global Islamic Research Network: integrated research, learning, source navigation, and reproducible research dossiers.
- V11 Open Islamic Learning Infrastructure: open schemas, export/import, interoperable learning records, reproducible datasets where rights permit, and community tooling.
- V12 Global Islamic Knowledge Commons: distributed, provenance-aware, rights-aware collaboration across libraries, researchers, teachers, and learners.

## V13–V20+: 2029–2030+
- V13 Knowledge Continuity Engine: long-term preservation, version lineage, citation permanence, source replacement detection, and institutional memory.
- V14 Living Scholarly Graph: continuously updated but review-gated knowledge graph with temporal versioning and explicit uncertainty.
- V15 Research Reproducibility Lab: reproducible search trails, evidence bundles, computational notebooks, dataset/version manifests, and claim-to-source audit.
- V16 Human–AI Scholar Workspace: side-by-side source reading, argument mapping, takhrij workspace, comparison matrices, teacher/scholar review queues, and collaborative annotation.
- V17 Federated Islamic Libraries Layer: interoperable discovery across independent collections without requiring a single centralized corpus; local ownership and rights remain authoritative.
- V18 Personal Knowledge & Mastery Memory: user-controlled lifelong learning memory, portable learner profile, local/offline operation, deletion/export controls, and privacy-preserving synchronization.
- V19 Collective Intelligence & Peer Review: anonymous or attributed peer review, disagreement maps, consensus/contested-state tracking, and evidence-weighted collaboration without turning consensus into truth.
- V20 Global Islamic Knowledge Observatory: longitudinal mapping of editions, manuscripts, translations, scholarly claims, curricula, learning outcomes, source gaps, and preservation risks.

## V21+ horizon
Explore only after evidence from V13–V20. Candidate areas include decentralized preservation, advanced multimodal archival reconstruction, machine-assisted manuscript collation, cross-corpus ontology alignment, simulation-based research training, and autonomous research agents operating under strict human approval and provenance constraints.

## Architecture rule
Future versions are additive layers over:
Acquisition → Validation → Rights → Provenance → Corpus → Knowledge → Evidence → Search → Learning → Research.

No future learning, agent, graph, or personalization layer may weaken the acquisition, rights, provenance, security, or protected-main boundaries.

## Evaluation gate
Every V5+ feature requires:
1. source/provenance tests;
2. rights tests;
3. security tests;
4. deterministic schema tests;
5. quality/effectiveness evaluation;
6. human review where authoritative interpretation is involved;
7. failure isolation;
8. rollback/fallback path;
9. documentation of known limitations.
