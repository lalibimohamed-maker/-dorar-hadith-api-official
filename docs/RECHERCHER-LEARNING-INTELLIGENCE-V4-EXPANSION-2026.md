# Rechercher Learning Intelligence V4 — 2026

This document extends the frozen learning-intelligence contract without changing the acquisition boundary.

## New direction

Rechercher becomes a source-grounded knowledge and learning substrate: verified corpus data can support discovery, understanding, practice, retrieval, transfer, assessment, review and mastery.

## V4 additions

- Explainable personalized learning paths using both prerequisite and similarity relations, so a blocked prerequisite does not unnecessarily stop learning.
- Learner knowledge-gap diagnosis and recovery recommendations.
- Multimodal learning across text, audio, image and structured knowledge where rights and validation permit it.
- Four graph layers: source graph, domain ontology, learning-resource graph, and learner-state graph.
- Evidence trails, provenance trails, contradiction-aware research, uncertainty and confidence calibration.
- Teach-back and Socratic learning modes.
- Research apprenticeship for source discovery, comparison and evidence evaluation.
- Cognitive-load controls and adaptive presentation.
- Transfer laboratory: apply knowledge to new but source-grounded cases rather than only repeating memorized answers.
- Continuous ablation/evaluation of learning strategies instead of assuming one scheduler or pedagogical method is universally optimal.
- Privacy-preserving learner state and offline-first resilience.
- Teacher/scholar review channel for authoritative interpretation.
- Cross-language terminology mapping and translation alignment while preserving source language and canonical Quran Arabic.
- Accessibility-adaptive presentation.

## Scientific rationale

Recent research supports explainable knowledge-graph-based personalization, learner-state graphs, multimodal educational knowledge graphs, and learning-path systems that combine prerequisite and similarity relations. These findings justify adding the capabilities as testable architecture, not as claims that every model is already superior in every Islamic-learning context.

## Islamic integrity

No V4 feature may:

1. alter the canonical Quran Arabic;
2. alter an acquired original PDF;
3. bypass provenance or rights controls;
4. turn AI-generated material into an authoritative religious ruling without appropriate human review;
5. erase madhhab distinctions or scholarly disagreement;
6. confuse existence of a source with authenticity of its contents;
7. stop or corrupt Rechercher PDF acquisition.

## Multilingual contract

The learning architecture remains language-neutral. Interface language, learning language and source language remain independent dimensions. A learner may ask in one language, learn in another, and inspect the original Arabic or other source language without replacing the source.

## Implementation rule

V4 is a frozen extension contract. Implementation proceeds in reviewed increments with tests and measurable evaluation. The acquisition engine remains independently protected.
