# Whole-encyclopedia catalog coverage

The Rechercher catalog registry spans the prophetic era, Companions and Followers, 1-400H, 401-800H, 801-1200H, 1201H onward, modern works, and future books.

Dorar `/v1/data/book` is a supplementary bibliographic index only. The encyclopedia's saved catalogs remain the discovery inputs, and new governed catalogs under `books-batches/**/catalog.json` are automatically included in future cycles.

No rights, redistribution, or PDF availability is inferred from a catalog or Dorar match.
