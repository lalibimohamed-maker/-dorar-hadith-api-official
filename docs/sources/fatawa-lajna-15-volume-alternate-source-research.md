# فتاوى اللجنة الدائمة — alternate-source research

## Status

This research establishes independent source candidates for the 15-volume collection. It does **not** by itself grant redistribution rights.

## Verified candidates

- Usul.ai: `فتاوى اللجنة الدائمة - المجموعة الأولى`; its catalog lists the collection and volumes, including volume 15. https://usul.ai/ar/t/fatawa-of-the-permanent-committee-volume-one/1
- جامع الكتب الإسلامية (KetabOnline): lists volumes 1–15, including `المجلد الخامس عشر (البيوع 3)`. https://ketabonline.com/ar/books/6018
- الجمهرة: lists the collection and explicitly lists `المجلد الخامس عشر من المجموعة الأولى: البيوع`. https://islamic-content.com/post/57547
- مكتبة مكنون: exposes searchable text for volume 15 and identifies it as `فتاوى اللجنة الدائمة - 1 (الجزء: 15)`. https://maknoon.org/ai/view.php?bk=fldbee15&p=472
- جامعة باتنة catalog: confirms a physical printed edition record for the collection, including edition/publisher metadata for the set. https://pmb.univ-batna.dz/

## Replacement rule

The acquisition branch must not silently substitute a different edition merely because the title matches. Before hosted replacement, verify for each volume:

1. title and collection identity;
2. compiler/editor identity;
3. publisher and edition/year;
4. volume number and ordering;
5. text/image integrity;
6. independent-source identity;
7. redistribution/license status;
8. SHA-256 (or equivalent) after acquisition.

If a source provides only web reading without redistribution permission, store metadata and canonical read URL rather than re-hosting the file.

## Current decision

The Waqfeya source should be treated as **replaceable at the source layer**, but no binary/PDF is to be replaced until all 15 volumes have a verified independent source and legal redistribution status. Partial substitution is preferable to fabricating completeness, and any missing volume remains explicitly marked as not independently verified.
