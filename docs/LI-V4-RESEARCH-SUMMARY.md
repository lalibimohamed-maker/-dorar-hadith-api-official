# Learning Intelligence v4 Research Summary

V4 introduces session memory, long-term learner memory, a bounded feedback buffer, cross-domain transfer routes, and pedagogical safety.

The design is hybrid and evidence-grounded. Verified corpus, provenance, rights and scholarly evidence remain authoritative. Adaptive components select sequence, modality, practice and scaffolding from learner evidence.

Session signals are summarized before durable persistence. Transfer is represented explicitly between source and target skills. Direct answers are constrained when retrieval effort should be preserved. Learning intelligence remains downstream of PDF acquisition and cannot block it.
